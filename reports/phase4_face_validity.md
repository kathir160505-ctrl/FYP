# Phase 4: Face validity of the four attacking styles

Proposal Section 3.12: cluster assignments are compared with the clubs' documented reputations and the literature's expectations. Typical clubs are those with the highest attacking dominance (PC1) in each cluster.

| Style | Clubs | Avg league finish | Possession % | Long-ball share % | Most typical clubs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Dominant Possession | 12 | 3.9 | 58.2 | 8.5 | Barcelona, Inter, Real Madrid, Manchester City, Juventus, Como |
| Controlled Build-up | 13 | 7.7 | 52.2 | 9.8 | Atlético Madrid, Napoli, Roma, Villarreal, AC Milan, Aston Villa |

## Expected from the literature

- **Dominant Possession:** Possession-heavy, high-volume attacks: the profile associated with clubs such as Barcelona and Manchester City (Gyarmati et al., 2014; Kempe et al., 2014).
- **Controlled Build-up:** Patient, accurate passing without dominating the ball: an intermediate profile between the possession and direct poles (Bekkers & Dabadghao, 2019).
- **Direct & Cross-Oriented:** Less possession, more long balls and crosses: the direct play described for much of the Premier League (Mitrotasios et al., 2019; Sarmento et al., 2013).
- **Long-Ball Low Block:** The most direct passing and lowest possession: the direct pole of the possession-versus-direct continuum (Kempe et al., 2014).

## Assessment

- The styles order cleanly along the possession-versus-direct continuum (Kempe et al., 2014): possession falls and long-ball share rises from Dominant Possession to Long-Ball Low Block.
- League standing follows the same order (average finish 3.9 → 7.7 → 12.7 → 16.9), consistent with possession dominance being associated with stronger clubs.
- The most typical Dominant Possession clubs include Barcelona and Manchester City, the possession archetypes in Gyarmati et al. (2014); Barcelona is also the most statistically unique club (DBSCAN), as the proposal expected.
- Contrary to the league expectations (Mitrotasios et al., 2019; Sarmento et al., 2013), league and style are not significantly associated (permutation p = 0.45); every style appears in every league. This is reported as a finding.
